A Family-Led Memorial

Continuing the Way She Lived

In memory of Deandrea Munoz Salmon

Founded by her three daughters, we turn the values she left with us into tangible opportunities to recognize people, strengthen families, and make a meaningful difference in the communities we call home.

Give Now Our Story
Who We Are

One life well lived, continuing through every person we reach

What began with one life well lived now continues through every person we have the opportunity to impact. We exist to turn the values Deedee left with us into tangible opportunities to recognize people, strengthen families, and meet real needs in the communities we call home.

What We Do

Three ways we carry her legacy forward

Scholarship

Celebrate Excellence

Honoring an outstanding female high school student-athlete each year, from the communities our family lives in and graduated from, with a scholarship that celebrates excellence in the classroom and in competition.

Back-to-School Blessing

Empower Families

Coming alongside single-parent households each back-to-school season with financial support to help lighten the load and start the year strong.

All Year Long

Live Generously

Meeting needs, big and small, throughout the year by showing up with generosity wherever an opportunity to make a difference arises.
