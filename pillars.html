What We Do

The Three Pillars

Each pillar carries its own color, so you can recognize which part of the work a page belongs to at a glance.

Scholarship

Celebrate Excellence

Honoring an outstanding Fort Stockton High School female student-athlete each year with a scholarship that celebrates excellence in the classroom and in competition. This pillar recognizes young women who represent the same discipline and heart Deedee valued, giving them a tangible boost as they head toward what's next.

Photo Coming Soon
Back-to-School Blessing

Empower Families

Coming alongside single-parent households each back-to-school season with financial support to help lighten the load and start the year strong. A new school year brings real costs, and this pillar exists to meet single parents in that moment with practical, direct help.

All Year Long

Live Generously

Meeting needs, big and small, throughout the year by showing up with generosity wherever an opportunity to make a difference arises. This pillar is the most flexible of the three, a standing commitment to say yes when the community needs something, whatever that looks like.

Behind the Pillars

Why These Three

Each pillar reflects something Deedee valued in her own life: pushing for excellence, showing up for families, and giving without waiting to be asked. Together, they turn one life well lived into a lasting pattern of generosity in Fort Stockton.

Support a Pillar That Speaks to You

Every gift, whether it's a golf tournament sponsorship, a recurring gift, or cheering on Miles for Mom, funds this work directly.
